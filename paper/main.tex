\title{\sysname: Topology-Aware Graph Contrastive Learning for Resilient Intrusion Detection}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Intrusion detectors must recognize evolving malicious behavior even when confirmed attack labels are scarce or incomplete. We present \sysname, a topology-aware graph contrastive learning framework for label-efficient intrusion detection. \sysname learns from benign system-activity graphs by combining message-passing and topological representations, then assigns anomaly scores in the same projected embedding space optimized by the contrastive objective. This design captures both local interactions and graph-scale structural deviations without using attack labels during representation learning. We evaluate \sysname on StreamSpot, GraSec-IoT, and Unicorn Wget against a supervised graph neural network and the benign-only GraphCL baseline. \sysname obtains F1 scores of 0.923, 0.790, and 0.583, respectively. Compared with GraphCL, this is higher on StreamSpot (0.450) and Unicorn Wget (0.203), but lower on GraSec-IoT (0.975). The supervised model leads on StreamSpot and GraSec-IoT, whereas \sysname has the highest F1 on Unicorn Wget. The mixed results show that topology-aware benign-only learning can improve detection in some graph settings but does not provide a uniform advantage.
\end{abstract}
